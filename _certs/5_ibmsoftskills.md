<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p>Develop key power skills to boost your work life. Master powerful people and soft skills for achieving professional success.</p>

<br />
<h2>Learning Objectives</h2>
<ol>
    <li>Collaborate Effectively for Professional Success</li>
    <ul>
        <li>Apply collaborative behaviors to help your team develop through the four stages of team development.</li>
        <li>Utilize different methods of collaboration to improve teamwork and cooperation in your workplace.</li>
        <li>Recognize the barriers to collaborating effectively and identify specific behaviors that enhance or damage collaboration.</li>
        <li>Identify techniques to effectively lead or participate in virtual team meetings.</li>
    </ul>
    <li>Present with Purpose: Create/Deliver Effective Presentations</li>
    <ul>
        <li>Create presentations that will leave a lasting impact on your audience.</li>
        <li>Present and deliver with confidence to engage your audience.</li>
        <li>Use techniques like storytelling, props, and discussion to enhance your presentations.</li>
        <li>Manage nerves effectively; Plan for and deal with unexpected problems that may arise.</li>
    </ul>
    <li>Delivering Quality Work with Agility</li>
    <ul>
        <li>Deliver high quality work that solves a problem and reduces effort for the person or group requesting it.</li>
        <li>Apply the quality work process to all requests through clear communication and focus on clients.</li>
        <li>Take ownership of your assignments and apply the five-step method that will help you deliver quality work.</li>
        <li>Work with agility while applying the quality work process to become a trusted advisor to your clients and colleagues.</li>
    </ul>
    <li>Solving Problems with Creative and Critical Thinking</li>
    <ul>
        <li>Utilize critical and creative thinking to solve issues.</li>
        <li>Describe the 5-step process of effectively solving problems.</li>
        <li>Analyze a problem and identify the root cause.</li>
        <li>Explore possible solutions and employ the problem solving process.</li>
    </ul>
    <li>Developing Interpersonal Skills</li>
    <ul>
        <li>Identify and analyze different communication styles and understand their strengths and weaknesses.</li>
        <li>Demonstrate active listening skills and questioning styles to explore issues and enhance understanding.</li>
        <li>Identify conflict response styles; approach and effectively mediate workplace conflicts.</li>
        <li>Determine ways to build and maintain trust and increase your influence and eminence in the workplace.</li>
    </ul>
    <li>People and Soft Skills Assessment</li>
    <ul>
        <li>Create and deliver presentations that will leave a lasting impact on your audience.</li>
        <li>Communicate with your peers in a way that builds your personal reputation and increases your eminence.</li>
        <li>Demonstrate active listening skills and questioning styles to explore issues, enhance understanding, and resolve problems.</li>
        <li>Complete work in an agile and effective manner that will ensure satisfaction and contribute to your career growth.</li>
    </ul>
</ol>
